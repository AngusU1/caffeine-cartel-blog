Winchester's Unmissable Coffee Scene

A journey through the best coffee shop in Winchester

Winchester is a city with a rich history and culture, but surprisingly it is forging its new path roasting some of the best coffee imaginable.

"Winchester Coffee Roasters"

Located in a business park in Winchester, Winchester Coffee Roasters is a small family-run business that has been roasting coffee for over a decade. Taking Australian coffee roasting knowledge and skills and bringing this to the south of England, Winchester Coffee Roasters has become a must-visit destination for coffee lovers.

More content after the image if needed.

Roasting

Stepping into the building in Winchester you will immediately encounter the 2 large roasting machines and a plethora of beans to purchase. From single origin blends to house blends there will definately be something for your preference. As someone who has tried many different offerings, my suggestions are:

  • Blended: Synergy
  • Single Origin: Colombian
  • Single Origin: Brazilian
  • Single Origin: Kenyan

If you cant grind the beans at home, the staff can perform this for you, just ask.

Classes

From professional barista training to coffee tasting classes, Winchester Coffee Roasters offers a range of classes to help you learn more about coffee and brewing. To someone picking up a new sage machine, the "Home Barista Coffee Course" is the perfect way to understand your new machine and produce great coffee at home
