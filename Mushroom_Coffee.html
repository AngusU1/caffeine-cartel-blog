Why Athletes and Entrepreneurs Are Switching To Mushroom Coffee

The Steven Bartlett Business Production

For decades, coffee has been the fuel of choice for early mornings, intense workouts, and long workdays. But recently, a new trend has been gaining traction among athletes, founders, and high performers: mushroom coffee.

From startup offices to gyms and training camps, more people are swapping their regular brew for coffee infused with functional mushrooms like Lion’s Mane, Cordyceps, and Reishi. But why the shift? Is mushroom coffee just another Steven Bartlett wellness fad, or does it offer real benefits for performance and productivity?

Let’s dive into the science behind mushroom coffee and explore why it’s becoming a go-to choice for athletes and entrepreneurs alike.

What Is Mushroom Coffee?

Mushroom coffee is typically made by combining regular coffee (or instant coffee) with powdered extracts of functional mushrooms. These mushrooms have been used for centuries in traditional medicine and are now studied for their potential effects on energy, focus, stress, and recovery.

Common mushrooms found in mushroom coffee include:

  • Lion’s Mane – Associated with focus and cognitive function
  • Cordyceps – Linked to physical performance and endurance
  • Reishi – Traditionally used for stress and recovery
  • Chaga – Known for antioxidant content
Typical Products On Offer

Sustained Energy Without the Crash

One of the most common complaints about regular coffee is the energy spike followed by a crash.

Athletes and entrepreneurs rely on consistent performance throughout the day—not short bursts followed by fatigue. Mushroom coffee often contains 30–50% less caffeine, which can mean:

  • Fewer jitters
  • More stable energy levels
  • Less dependence on multiple cups per day

Cordyceps, in particular, is popular among athletes because it’s traditionally associated with oxygen utilisation and stamina, making it appealing for workouts or long training sessions.

Improved Focus For Deep Work And Training

Entrepreneurs need hours of deep focus, while athletes rely on mental clarity during training and competition.

Lion’s Mane mushroom is one of the biggest draws here. It’s often linked to brain health, memory, and concentration, which is why many founders are experimenting with Lion’s Mane coffee blends.

Many Users Report

  • Smoother mental clarity
  • Less mental fogg
  • Better focus without overstimulation

Better Stress Management And Recovery

Entrepreneurs juggle deadlines, decisions, and pressure. Athletes face physical strain, cortisol spikes, and recovery demands. Mushroom coffee blends often include adaptogenic mushrooms like Reishi, which are traditionally used to help the body adapt to stress.

Potential benefits people look for include:

  • Calmer energy
  • Reduced nervousness compared to strong coffee
  • Support for post-training recovery

This makes mushroom coffee especially attractive for afternoon use, when regular coffee can interfere with sleep or increase anxiety.

Is Mushroom Coffee Right For Everyone?

Mushroom coffee isn’t a magic solution, and it’s not for everyone.

If you love the strong buzz of traditional coffee or rely on very high caffeine intake, mushroom coffee may feel too mild. Additionally, anyone with medical conditions or mushroom sensitivities should avoid.

That said, for athletes and entrepreneurs looking to optimise focus, energy, and recovery, mushroom coffee offers an interesting alternative worth exploring.

Cartel Thoughts

The rise of mushroom coffee isn’t just about trends—it reflects a shift in how high performers think about energy.

Athletes want endurance without burnout. Entrepreneurs want focus without anxiety. Mushroom coffee sits right at that intersection, offering a smoother, more balanced way to stay sharp and energised.
